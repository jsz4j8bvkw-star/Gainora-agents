Questions

Good to know

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Book a free consultation. Mirela talks through your skin concerns and goals and recommends what suits you.

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Some treatments need one first, to check suitability and your medical history. It can often happen on the same day as the treatment.

Are the treatments painful?

Most involve only mild discomfort. For some, numbing cream can be used to keep you comfortable.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment and your goals. Some clients see a difference after one session; others benefit from a course.

Is there any downtime?

It varies. Some treatments have little or none; others can leave temporary redness, swelling or sensitivity.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes, some work well together. Mirela will advise on suitable combinations and timing.
