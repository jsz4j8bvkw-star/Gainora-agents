Privacy

Privacy

What the contact form collects

Your name, your phone number and email (if you give them), the treatment you’re interested in and your message. We also note which page you sent it from and how you found the website, such as Google or Instagram.

What it’s used for

Your message goes to Alder Ease Aesthetics (Mirela Borowska) so she can reply to you. It isn’t sold or used for anything else. Gainora, who runs this website, stores it securely on Mirela’s behalf.

Visit statistics

The website counts visits and taps on the phone number and booking buttons, without identifying you, to see how the website is doing. If Google tools are switched on, you’ll be asked first.

Bookings

Online bookings are made through Booksy, which has its own privacy policy.

Seeing or deleting your details

To see what we hold about you, or to ask for it to be deleted, email aldereaseaesthetics@gmail.com or call 07922 509893.
