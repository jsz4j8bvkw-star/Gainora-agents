About

Meet Mirela

Mirela Borowska is the founder of Alder Ease Aesthetics in Crewe. Level 2, 3 and 4 aesthetics trained, fully insured, and in practice for over 3 years.

Why I started

I started my aesthetics business because I have always had a passion for beauty and wanted to make a positive difference in how people look and feel about themselves.

I take pride in my attention to detail, high standards of work and genuine care for every client. What makes me most proud is seeing my clients leave feeling happy, confident and comfortable in their own skin.

My goal has always been to create a welcoming, relaxing environment where clients feel valued, cared for and genuinely enjoy coming back for their treatments.

Mirela

What makes Alder Ease different

A personal experience, where every client has Mirela’s full attention, rather than simply coming in for a treatment.

  • Time for you

    Appointments are never rushed. Mirela listens, understands what you want, and makes sure you feel comfortable and cared for.

  • Care in every detail

    Every treatment is carried out with precision and tailored to your skin and your goals.

  • Honest advice

    You’ll know what each treatment involves and what to expect, so you can decide with confidence.

  • A warm, relaxed room

    A friendly, easy-going atmosphere where you can chat, unwind and enjoy your appointment.

  • Support afterwards

    Written aftercare, and Mirela is there to answer questions by phone or message once you’ve gone home.
