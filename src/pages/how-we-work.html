Answers first. Then you decide.

Our work is split into a diagnosis and, only if you want it, implementation. Here is what happens at each stage and what we'll ask of you.

  1. You tell us what isn't working

    Send an enquiry with your business, your website if you have one, and what you believe is going wrong. Rough is fine.

    • Business name and website
    • The problem or concern
    • Systems you currently use
    • What you'd like to be different

  2. We agree the scope and price

    A short conversation to understand the situation. We then confirm in writing what we'll investigate, what information we'll need, the price and the timeline.

    Nothing starts until you've agreed it.

  3. We investigate

    Depending on the scope, we may look at:

    • Website performance and customer journey
    • Sales and enquiry handling
    • Business processes and workflows
    • Existing systems and reporting
    • Product presentation
    • Relevant competitors
    • Business data you share with us

    We only access private information with your authorisation.

  4. You receive the report

    Findings, the evidence for each, priorities, recommended solutions and practical next steps, including the implementation options. We walk you through it and answer your questions.

  5. You decide what happens next

    Use the report yourself, give it to your team or another supplier, or ask us to implement some or all of the recommendations. There's no obligation and no follow-up pressure.

  6. If you hire us: build, review, implement

    • Agree the scope and price of the work
    • Build or configure the solution
    • Test it
    • Present it to you for review
    • Make changes live only with your approval
    • Measure the outcome where reliable data exists

The rules we work by

  • Authorised access only

    We only look at private systems and data you've given us permission to see, and only for the agreed work.

  • Facts kept separate

    Reports show what we observed, what data supports, what's a likely cause and what still needs checking.

  • No surprise changes

    Nothing is changed in your live systems without your approval. Changes are tested before they go live.

  • Honest measurement

    Where we measure results, we tell you what changed, including when a change made no measurable difference.

  • Clear prices

    Scope and price are agreed in writing before work begins. No open-ended billing.

  • Checked by a person

    We use modern tools, including AI, to work efficiently. A person reviews every finding and every change before you see it.
