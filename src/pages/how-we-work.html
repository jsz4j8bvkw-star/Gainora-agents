A short call. A clear plan. Then it runs.

Every client follows the same four stages, whichever package they take. Here's what happens at each one and what we'll ask of you.

  1. A 15-minute diagnostic call

    You tell us what's happening and what you've tried. We ask the questions that matter and tell you which package fits, or whether none does. No pitch, no obligation.

    • Your business and website, if you have one
    • How enquiries come in and what happens to them
    • What you'd like to be different

    Book the call

  2. A plan, if you need one

    When the right fix is obvious, you can start straight away. When it isn't, the {{prices.audit}} Business Audit finds out and writes it down: what we saw, what it's costing you, what to fix in what order. Take a package within {{prices.auditCreditDays}} days and the audit is credited in full.

  3. Your package goes live

    We agree the price and the start date in writing, then set it up with you. Depending on the package:

    • The receptionist learns your services, prices, areas, hours and diary, and you approve its script
    • The website, Google Business Profile and review system go up in your name
    • Command Centre starts watching your website, reviews and competitors
    • For the Partner, the fix plan is built, tested and handed over

    Nothing reaches a customer or goes live in your systems without your approval.

  4. It keeps running, and we keep improving it

    Every month you get the numbers: calls answered, enquiries caught out of hours, jobs booked, reviews gained, what the ads brought in. We tune what the numbers say needs tuning. Ongoing care is included in every package.

    You own the website, the phone number, the Google profile and the data, and you keep them if you ever leave.

The rules we work by

  1. Honest with your customers

    The receptionist never claims to be a person and says so if asked. Callers are told when a call is recorded. Every message a customer gets is one you've switched on, approved or written.

  2. Authorised access only

    We only look at systems and data you've given us permission to see, and only for the agreed work.

  3. No surprise changes

    Nothing changes in your live systems, your website or your phone line without your approval. Changes are tested first.

  4. Honest measurement

    We tell you what changed, including when a change made no measurable difference.

  5. Fixed prices, in writing

    One price per package, agreed before work begins. No open-ended billing.

  6. Checked by a person

    We use modern tools, including AI, to work quickly. A person reviews every script, every finding and every change before you see it.

What we don't do

A few things we're asked about and say no to, so you know where we stand.

  • We don't send marketing messages to people who haven't asked for them. Review requests go only to your own customers, and always with a way to opt out.
  • We don't give legal, accounting, tax or regulatory advice. When a finding points there, we say so and suggest the right specialist.
  • We don't lock you in. Your website, number, profile and data are yours.
  • We don't recommend a package because it leads to more work for us. "None of them" is an answer we give.
