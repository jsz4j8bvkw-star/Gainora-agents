Why we work this way

The same rules apply to every package, every client and every month.

  1. Outcomes, not tools.

    You're buying answered calls and booked jobs, not software. We use modern tools, including AI, and a person checks what they do.

  2. Honest with your customers.

    The receptionist never claims to be a person, and says so if asked. Every message to a customer is one you've switched on, approved or written.

  3. Fixed prices, in writing.

    One price per package, agreed before anything starts. No open-ended billing.

  4. You own everything.

    Your website, domain, phone number, Google profile and data stay in your name, and leave with you if you go.

  5. Measured, including when it doesn't work.

    Monthly numbers, and we tell you when a change made no difference.

  6. No pushing.

    The audit stands on its own. What happens next is your decision.
