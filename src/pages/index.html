Get a business diagnosis How we work

Most business problems have a cause. Finding it is the first step.

Owners usually know something is wrong long before they know why. These are the kinds of situations people bring to us.

  • Your website isn't turning visitors into enquiries.
  • Important follow-ups get missed.
  • Your team repeats the same manual tasks every week.
  • Your systems don't talk to each other.
  • Your reports don't give you a clear picture.
  • Customers struggle with processes that should be simple.

Sometimes the cause is obvious once someone looks properly. Sometimes it's somewhere you weren't looking at all. Either way, it's cheaper to understand it before you spend money fixing it.

Understand the problem before paying to fix it.

A Business Diagnosis is a focused investigation into one area of your business that isn't working as it should. You get a report you can act on yourself, with your own team, or with us.

  • Evidence behind each finding, with what we observed kept separate from what still needs confirming
  • Priorities, so you know which problems are worth solving first
  • Recommended solutions in plain language
  • Next steps you can take with or without us

You get answers first. You decide what happens next.

Four stages, with a clear handover at the end. Nothing about the diagnosis depends on you hiring us afterwards.

  1. Tell us about your business

    What's happening, what you've tried and what you'd like to be different. We agree what to investigate and the price.

  2. We investigate

    We examine the evidence you authorise us to see, research the problem and work out the likely causes.

  3. You receive the report

    Clear findings, the evidence behind them, priorities and recommendations, walked through with you.

  4. You decide what's next

    Act on it yourself, give it to your team, or ask us to help implement some or all of it.

Implementation is optional. The report is yours to use however you like. We'll never make a recommendation just because it leads to more work for us.

What a solution can look like

Illustrative problem-and-solution scenarios showing the kind of work we do. They are examples, not past client projects.

Missed enquiries

Problem
Enquiries arrive by email, phone and web form, and some are never answered.
Solution
One place where every enquiry is captured, assigned and followed up, with reminders when something is waiting.

Manual reporting

Problem
Someone spends a day each month copying figures between spreadsheets.
Solution
An automated report that pulls the figures together into a dashboard the whole team can read.

Poor website experience

Problem
Visitors arrive but can't easily find what they need or how to take the next step.
Solution
A clearer customer journey with fewer obstacles and an obvious next action on every key page.

Disconnected systems

Problem
The same customer details are typed into three different tools.
Solution
An integration that passes information between tools automatically and removes the manual handoff.

Why we work this way

  • We prioritise what matters.

    Some problems aren't worth the cost of fixing. We'll tell you which.

  • We build practical solutions.
