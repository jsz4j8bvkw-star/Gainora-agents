How the calculator works

Simple arithmetic, shown in full, so you can disagree with any step.

  • Capture & Convert: your enquiries a week × the share by phone × the share unanswered × the share who leave no message = calls lost today. Between half and all of those become enquiries once every call is answered; at your conversion rate and average job value, that's the monthly figure. The tier is chosen from your call minutes.
  • Launch & Grow: your monthly website visits × the published visit-to-enquiry rate for your industry = enquiries from a site you own, converted at your rate, plus whatever you currently pay lead sites.
  • Command Centre (included in every package): your weekly admin hours × 4.33 = hours a month. We assume a quarter to a half comes back, valued at your hourly rate.
  • Growth & Operations Partner: shown for teams of 6 or more. Admin plus team coordination at the UK average, as hours a month. The audit replaces this with your real number.

Two things we looked for and could not find a credible published UK figure for: how many enquiries become customers in each industry, and how many callers go to a competitor after an unanswered call. Both are marked as estimates for you to replace. If you know of a good source, tell us.
