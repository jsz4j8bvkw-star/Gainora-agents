How it works

  1. Setup call. We learn your services, areas, prices, opening hours, what counts as urgent, and how you like to book. The receptionist's script is written from your answers, and you approve it.
  2. Calls go to the receptionist first, or only when you don't answer or you're closed. Your choice, and you can change it any time.
  3. Every call becomes an enquiry in your inbox with the details, a summary and what the receptionist promised. Urgent ones are texted to your mobile straight away.
  4. Follow-ups send themselves. Thank-yous, quote chasers, payment reminders and review requests go out on the right day. You decide which are on, what they say, and which ones should ask you first.
  5. Every month you see the numbers and we tune what needs tuning.

Honest by design

  • The receptionist never claims to be a person. If a caller asks, it says so.
  • If calls are recorded, callers are told at the start.
  • Emergencies follow rules you agree in advance: for example, a gas smell is told to call the gas emergency line first; a clinic caller describing a red-flag symptom is told to call 999 or NHS 111.
  • Customers only ever get messages you've switched on, approved or written. Review requests always include a way to opt out.
  • Texting stops the moment a customer replies, and never before 8am or after 8pm unless it's urgent.

What you'll need

A mobile number for alerts, your diary or booking system if you want bookings made, and about an hour for the setup call. We connect to the common booking systems; if yours isn't one of them, the receptionist offers times and we confirm them with you.
