Questions about the audit

Do I need the audit before a package?

No. If the 15-minute call makes the right package obvious, we'll say so and you can start straight away. The audit is for when it isn't obvious, or when you want the evidence first.

How is the credit applied?

Take any package within {{prices.auditCreditDays}} days of receiving the audit and {{prices.audit}} comes off your first invoice. It's a credit against the package, not a refund.

Can you audit my business without private data?

Partly. We can review everything public and make test calls and enquiries. But public information can't tell us your real enquiry numbers, so findings based only on it are clearly marked as observations to verify.

What if the honest answer is that nothing's wrong?

Then that's what the audit says. It happens, and it's useful to know.

What won't the audit cover?

Legal, accounting, tax or regulatory advice. If a finding points to one of those areas, the audit says so and recommends the right specialist.

How is my information protected?

We only access what you authorise, use it only for the audit, keep it secure and delete it when the work ends or when you ask. See the privacy policy.
