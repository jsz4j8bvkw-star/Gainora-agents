How we decide whether we can help

We assess feasibility before making any commitment, and we're honest when something isn't a good fit for us.

  1. Understand the problem

    What's happening, who it affects, what you've tried and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. Check feasibility

    Whether it can be solved with the time, budget and systems available, and whether we're the right people to do it.

  3. Propose a solution

    A written proposal with the approach, scope, price, timeline and what we'll need from you.

  4. Build, test and hand over

    If you go ahead, we build in agreed stages, test with you and document how it works.

What we won't promise

We won't claim we can solve every kind of business problem.

If your problem needs specialist expertise we don't have, such as legal, accounting, regulated financial advice or heavy infrastructure engineering, we'll tell you early and, where we can, point you to the right kind of specialist. If we think a cheaper or simpler option would do the job, we'll say that too.
