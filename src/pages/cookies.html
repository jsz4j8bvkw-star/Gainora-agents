Cookies

Draft for review. This document is a starting point, not legal advice. It must be completed and checked before the website is published.

Cookies we set

This website doesn't set any cookies of its own and doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The business improvement calculator runs in your browser and stores nothing.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies.

The booking page embeds Google Calendar's appointment booking. When you use it, Google may set cookies needed to make the booking work, and Google's privacy policy applies. If you'd rather not, the same calendar opens in a new tab from the button on that page.

When you submit a form, it's processed by our website host Netlify to deliver it to us.

If this changes

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting them, as UK law requires.
