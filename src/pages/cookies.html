Cookies we use

As built, this website doesn't set any cookies and doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That's why there's no cookie banner.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies, but you can read Google's privacy policy for details.

When you submit a form, it's processed by our website host Netlify to deliver it to us.

If this changes

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting them, as UK law requires.
