Enquiries arrive through three channels, but only the web form feeds the shared inbox. Phone messages and direct emails depend on whoever receives them. The highest-value change is a single place to capture every enquiry, which can be done with the firm's existing tools.

Questions about the diagnosis

What exactly will you investigate?

The area we agree together at the start, for example how enquiries are handled or how customers move through your website. The scope is written down before work begins, so you know what's included and what isn't.

What information do you need from me?

It depends on the scope. Typically a conversation about the problem, plus access to relevant information such as website analytics, process descriptions, examples of reports or a walkthrough of a system. We ask only for what the investigation needs.

Can you review my business without private data?

Partly. We can review anything that's publicly visible, such as your website and customer journey. But public information can't tell us your real conversion rates, revenue or internal processes, so findings based only on public information are clearly marked as observations or hypotheses to verify.

What will I actually receive?

A written report with an executive summary, the scope, findings with their evidence, priorities, recommendations and next steps. We walk you through it so you can ask questions.

How long will the diagnosis take?

We agree the timeline when we agree the scope. It depends on the area and on how quickly the information we need becomes available. We'd rather give you a realistic date than a fast one we can't keep.

What if the problem needs specialist expertise?

We'll tell you. We don't give legal, accounting, tax or regulatory advice. If a finding points to one of those areas, the report says so and recommends getting the right specialist.

Do I have to use your implementation services?

No. The diagnosis stands on its own. Many clients will implement the recommendations themselves or with their existing team.

Can you build a custom solution?

Often, yes. If the recommended fix is something we can build, such as a website change, an automation or an internal tool, we can quote for it separately. See custom solutions.

How will my information be protected?

We only access what you authorise, use it only for the agreed work, keep it secure and delete it when the work ends or when you ask. Read our privacy policy for details.
